Set during the twilight of the Clone Wars, the game follows former Republic officer Hawks and an unconventional squad opposing the Infinite Coil.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. This trainer enhances the game's squad-based tactical systems by adjusting operator health, action points, Advantage points, injuries, and campaign resources. It is designed for single-player missions, operations, and base-management activities while preserving the game's turn-based structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the campaign, experiment with squad compositions, test tactical abilities, reduce the impact of injuries and permadeath, or enjoy a more forgiving single-player experience.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement takes place during operator turns and consumes action points. Use the unlimited action points option when you want to reposition freely, reach cover, or test different approaches before committing to an attack. The game displays movement range and AP costs, so plan routes around cover and mission objectives.

### Tools & Inventory

Operators can be customized with different appearances, loadouts, combat specializations, and abilities. Prepare the squad at the Den before deployment, then use the trainer's campaign resources to support experimentation with available equipment and team configurations.

### World & Environment

The Den is Zero Company's base of operations and contains the Holotable used to choose missions and non-combat operations. Missions advance the campaign cycle, while operations consume Intel and can provide items, credits, influence, information, and relationship changes. Resource options are most useful between deployments when managing the squad and selecting activities.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based. Each operator normally receives three action points to move, attack, use abilities, call for backup, enter overwatch, or rally a downed teammate. Advantage points power special actions and are earned by dealing damage. Use the trainer to maintain health, AP, Advantage, or injury protection while testing cover, coordinated attacks, reaction fire, and different operator synergies.
